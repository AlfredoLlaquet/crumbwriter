Crumb Writer for macOS

Copy Crumb Writer to Applications, then open it from there. In Finder the keyboard equivalent of dragging is Command+C on Crumb Writer, Shift+Command+A to open Applications, and Command+V to paste.

This universal app runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 15 or later. The release includes a GitHub-signed build record. The app uses a local ad hoc signature. Apple has not notarized it. After trying to open it, macOS may offer an Open Anyway button in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Use that per-app option only after checking the download came from the official release.

https://github.com/AlfredoLlaquet/crumbwriter/releases/latest
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

To update, quit Crumb Writer and replace its copy in Applications. To uninstall, quit the editor and move the app to the Trash. Manuscripts, companion files, and personal settings are stored separately.
