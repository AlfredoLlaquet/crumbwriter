Crumb Writer's Windows PDF text reader

The editor runs crumbwriter-pdftext.exe as a separate process. It passes the
PDF through standard input and reads UTF-8 text from standard output. The
installation includes Poppler's encoding maps under share/poppler.

The Windows source download includes this file, pdftext.cpp, the project's
Unlicense, and the exact MSYS2 Poppler source, patches and build recipes.
The reader linked with Poppler is distributed under Poppler's GPL terms.
Its original notices are installed in the third-party directory.

In an MSYS2 UCRT64 terminal with GCC, pkgconf and Poppler installed, build
the reader from the directory containing pdftext.cpp with this command.

c++ -std=c++17 -O2 pdftext.cpp -o crumbwriter-pdftext.exe $(pkg-config --cflags --libs poppler-cpp)

The project's CMake build also creates and installs the reader automatically.
