## What’s new in 3.0

- Keep several manuscripts open in tabs. Switching tabs or moving one to a new window preserves its caret, selection, undo history and writing tools. **Ctrl+Tab** switches manuscripts. **Ctrl+Shift+T** reopens a recently closed tab.
- Organize prose with four heading levels. On the line below a heading, type `=`, `-`, `~` or `.`. The Titles panel follows your place in the outline. Bullets and numbered, lettered or Roman-numeral lists continue as editable text.
- In Generic mode, **Ctrl+K** inserts the opening or closing guillemet. A passage such as `«in passing»` appears in italics as you move on, and the marks stay in the UTF-8 manuscript. Manuscript Settings can display the marks literally for a particular document.
- **Compare with Startup Backup** shows changed paragraphs side by side. **F3** and **Shift+F3** move between changes. The comparison is read-only, so you can return to the same writing session with your draft intact.
- FreeBSD 14.x gains its own amd64 package. The downloads also include FreeBSD 15.x, Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04 LTS, Debian 13, Windows and macOS. Each installer carries the application version in its filename.
