In Generic mode, place an `=` marker below the first line or choose **Main Title** to give it the largest type. Main Title shares the behavior and outline level of **Title 1**. An unmarked first line is regular text, and blank lines have their ordinary height. Older manuscripts that relied on automatic title detection can be marked with **Main Title**.

The Style and Merriam-Webster shortcuts now accept both ordinary keystrokes and text delivered by an input method. After **Ctrl+Shift+K**, a digit applies a style. After **Ctrl+Shift+L**, **D**, **S**, or **T** opens the selected reference. **Escape** cancels either prompt.
